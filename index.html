THE SMILE EDIT

Small habits. Healthier smiles.

Welcome to Smile Magazine, your little guide to everyday oral health.

DAILY ROUTINE

Two minutes, twice a day.

Brush gently for two minutes, morning and evening. Use fluoride toothpaste and remember to clean every surface.

BETWEEN THE TEETH

Don't forget to floss.

Interdental brushes or dental floss help clean the spaces your toothbrush cannot reach.

FOOD & WELLBEING

Make water your favourite drink.

Water helps rinse away food particles. Limit frequent sugary snacks and drinks to protect your teeth.

FAMILY CARE

Start healthy habits early.

Help children brush regularly and make dental visits a positive part of growing up.

General educational information, not a substitute for individual dental advice.
